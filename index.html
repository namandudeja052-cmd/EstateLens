About the research project

House Price Prediction Using Statistical Machine Learning

Musky Kumar, Naman Dudeja and Aditya Verma studied approximately 187,531 listings across 81 Indian cities. The team cleaned missing values, engineered features, scaled numerical inputs and encoded categories.

Using an 80/20 training and test split, they compared Linear Regression, Decision Tree Regression and Random Forest Regression. The project reports that Random Forest performed best, with R² of approximately 0.926 and mean absolute error of approximately ₹10.41 lakh.
